# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Recruiters and hiring managers evaluating Jakob Kjeldsteen for software development roles. They need to assess his work and decide whether to download his résumé or contact him.

## Product Purpose

Showcase Jakob's software development work through professional projects, playable games, and development logs. The portfolio succeeds when a hiring visitor can understand what Jakob contributed and take the next step through his résumé or contact page.

## Positioning

Jakob's work spans industrial digital twins and simulation, backend systems, and independent game development. Professional project writeups and the playable Epoch Raiders project show how he applies those skills in different settings.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Visitors can browse work, games, devlogs, background, and skills. They can download a PDF résumé and send a message through the contact form. Form delivery depends on EmailJS configuration.

## Evidence on Hand

- The Work page documents professional and personal projects.
- The Games and Devlogs pages provide playable projects and accounts of development decisions.
- `src/resources/resume.pdf` contains the downloadable résumé. `src/resources/images/` contains the profile photo and gameplay assets.

## Product Principles

- Make it easy for hiring visitors to find relevant work and understand Jakob's contributions.
- Support technical claims with project details, playable builds, and development notes where available.
- Keep the résumé and contact options easy to find after visitors review the work.
